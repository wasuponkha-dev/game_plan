| 3 | Kabuterimon → 20 → Angemon → 50 | MagnaAngemon |
| 4 | MagnaAngemon → 30 | **Devimon** |
| 5 | Devimon → 40 → Myotismon → 99 | **MaloMyotismon** |
| 6 | Growlmon → 40 → WarGrowlmon → 99 | **Gallantmon** |
